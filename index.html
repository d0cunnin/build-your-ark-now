Practical Readiness

Prepare Your Home & Family

Readiness begins at home. These are the foundational areas every household should address.

Food

A meaningful supply of shelf-stable food protects your family when access to groceries is disrupted.

Water

Water is the most critical resource. Store, filter, and plan for sustained access.

Power & Lighting

Outages happen. Backup power, flashlights, and communication devices keep your household functioning.

Medical Supplies

A well-stocked first aid kit and a supply of essential medications can be life-changing in an emergency.

Communication

Know how to reach your household members when phones and internet are unavailable.

Planning

A clear household plan — meeting points, contacts, roles — removes confusion when it matters most.

Visionary &
Author
The Visionary

D'Andrea Bolden

D'Andrea Bolden is the visionary behind Build Your Ark. Following two powerful and distinct dreams instructing her to create a survival and preparedness manual, she began developing what would become It's Time to Build Your Ark.

What began as a call to create a practical survival manual has grown into something much larger: an online hub designed to equip individuals, leaders, families, churches, and communities with the knowledge, resources, tools, and strategies needed to prepare for the days ahead.

Build Your Ark was created from the belief that preparation is not about fear. It is about wisdom, stewardship, faith, and responsibility. Just as Noah was instructed to prepare before the rain came, we are called to recognize the importance of preparation before we find ourselves in a moment of crisis.

Through education, practical resources, biblical principles, and community-centered preparation, Build Your Ark seeks to help people prepare physically, mentally, spiritually, financially, practically, and collectively.

"Your ark may not look like someone else's. But whatever God has entrusted to you, it is worth preparing to protect."
