Founder's Word

So many warnings have gone out telling people to prepare, but many have been left with awareness and no instruction.


People can feel that something is shifting, but they do not know what to do, what to build, or how to move.


After having two dreams about creating a manual that gives instruction, hope, and clarity, I knew this was not something to ignore. The assignment was not just to warn — but to show people how to prepare and what to build.


I will be honest — I was apprehensive about putting this manual together. I said, "God, I am going to look really strange to people." But obedience is better than sacrifice.


Scripture says to consider the ant and be wise. The ant does not wait. It prepares. Noah did not wait either. God warned him, and he built the ark before the rain came.


This is not the season to keep hearing. This is the season to respond. It is time to move from warning to preparation, from waiting to building, and from reacting to positioning.

— D'Andrea Bolden

The Manual

A 400-Page Strategic Preparedness Manual

It's Time to Build Your Ark is a comprehensive guide that combines practical guidance, spiritual insight, and wisdom to help you prepare, think strategically, and move with intention.

Rooted in the wisdom of the ant and the example of Noah, this guide equips you to move from warning to preparation, from waiting to building, and from reacting to positioning.

  • Develop essential preparedness systems
  • Strengthen your situational awareness
  • Build aligned, intentional community
  • Prepare wisely for uncertain times
  • Move with foresight instead of fear

The ark is built in obedience ahead of time.
